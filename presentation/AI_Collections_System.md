# AI-Powered Collections System

## Executive Briefing

### Tata iQ / Geldium GenAI Case Study — Forage Simulation

---

## 1. How the System Works

The proposed system follows a four-stage workflow:

### 1. Customer Data

The system uses relevant customer information including:

- Payment history
- Missed payments
- Credit utilization
- Credit score
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Income
- Account tenure

### 2. Risk Assessment

The system:

- Identifies risk indicators
- Estimates delinquency risk
- Assigns an initial risk profile
- Flags uncertain cases for review

### 3. Personalized Action

Based on the risk profile:

**Low Risk**
- Routine monitoring

**Medium Risk**
- Payment reminders
- Supportive outreach

**High Risk**
- Prioritized human review
- Appropriate customer-support options

### 4. Learning Loop

The system continuously:

- Tracks outcomes
- Measures intervention effectiveness
- Monitors accuracy
- Evaluates fairness
- Identifies areas for improvement

---

## 2. Decision Logic

The system uses multiple risk indicators rather than relying on a single variable.

### Example

Repeated missed payments + elevated credit utilization

↓

Risk assessment

↓

Explain key risk factors

↓

Generate personalized support recommendation

↓

Human review

↓

Customer action

↓

Measure outcome

↓

Improve the system

---

## 3. Role of Agentic AI

### Autonomous Activities

The AI can:

- Monitor risk signals
- Prioritize cases
- Generate recommendations
- Draft routine communications
- Summarize risk factors
- Detect anomalies
- Monitor performance indicators

### Human Oversight

Humans should:

- Approve consequential actions
- Review high-risk cases
- Override inappropriate recommendations
- Approve sensitive communications
- Investigate complex cases
- Handle exceptions
- Make governance decisions

### Guiding Principle

> Automate routine analysis and recommendations while keeping humans in control of high-impact decisions.

---

## 4. Responsible AI Guardrails

### Fairness

- Monitor performance across relevant customer groups
- Compare false-positive and false-negative rates
- Investigate unexplained disparities
- Monitor potential proxy variables

### Explainability

- Show key factors behind risk assessments
- Provide plain-language explanations
- Allow human reviewers to challenge recommendations

### Privacy and Compliance

- Protect customer financial information
- Apply appropriate access controls
- Maintain audit trails
- Follow applicable financial and data-protection requirements

### Human Oversight

- No autonomous approval or rejection decisions
- Escalate uncertain cases
- Allow human override
- Continuously monitor AI performance

---

## 5. Expected Business Impact

### Business KPIs

The proposed pilot would aim to achieve:

- At least 10% relative reduction in delinquency within the targeted intervention group
- Improved identification of customers requiring early support
- Better prioritization of collections resources
- Reduced manual risk-analysis effort
- Continuous monitoring of accuracy and fairness

These are pilot targets and should be validated against a comparable control group.

### Customer Outcomes

- Earlier access to appropriate support
- More personalized communications
- Fewer unnecessary collection interventions
- More consistent treatment
- Clearer explanations
- Greater transparency and trust

---

## 6. Responsible AI Principle

The proposed system is designed around:

**Predict → Explain → Recommend → Human Review → Act → Learn**

AI should support human decision-making rather than replace it when decisions could have significant financial consequences for customers.

---

## 7. Executive Takeaway

> **Scale collections through responsible automation — not automation without accountability.**
